Soft paper-craft semi-flat 2D illustration for an empty state in a work-hours tracking
app. Subjects, and nothing else: one simple stopwatch with a completely blank white face
(no numerals, no digits, only two simple hands), one blank calendar page with empty grid
cells, one small checklist card with empty tick rows, and one small card with a simple
rising zig-zag trend line. Exactly these four paper objects, no extra balls, spheres,
dots or floating shapes.
Style: gentle light paper-craft layers, semi-flat 2D, flat colors with crisp edges, same
calm flat style family as a matching medication empty-state illustration. Mostly flat,
only very shallow paper depth.
Color palette strictly limited to: white, soft misty blue #9DB8E8, deep blue #3168D5,
mint green #6CBFA3, small coral #E27A72 accents.
Composition: horizontal 4:3, the four objects grouped in the center, generous 20% empty
safe margin all around.
Shadows: only tiny soft shadows between overlapping paper elements; absolutely no shadow
cast onto the background.
Background: one single completely flat solid magenta #FF00FF across the entire canvas,
perfectly uniform like a chroma-key screen, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no
darker tones, no rose or purple tint — pure vivid magenta only.
Strictly forbidden: any text, letters, numbers, clock numerals, currency symbols, human
figures, logos, brands, watermarks, gradient orbs, bokeh, spheres, complex background.
