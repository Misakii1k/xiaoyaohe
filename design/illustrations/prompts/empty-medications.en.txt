Soft paper-craft semi-flat 2D illustration for an empty state in a medication management
app. Subjects: one simple weekly pill organizer box with seven empty compartments, one
blank calendar page with empty grid cells only, and one small round check-mark token
leaning nearby. Conveys "no medications added yet", calm and trustworthy.
Style: gentle light paper-craft layers, semi-flat 2D, clean and friendly, soft rounded
shapes. Color palette strictly: white, soft misty blue #9DB8E8 and #3168D5, mint green
#6CBFA3, a small touch of coral #E27A72.
Composition: horizontal 4:3, subjects grouped in the center, generous 20% empty safe
margin on all sides, must stay readable when displayed only 240 pixels wide.
Shadows: only very short, light shadows between the paper elements themselves; the
elements must NOT cast any shadow onto the background.
Background: one single completely flat solid magenta #FF00FF filling the whole canvas,
absolutely uniform, no gradient, no texture, no vignette, no floor.
Strictly no text, no letters, no numbers, no logo, no human figures, no medicine brand,
no red cross, no watermark, no gradient orbs, no bokeh, no complex background scenery.
